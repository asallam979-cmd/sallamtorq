THE BRAND

BUILT WITH
PURPOSE.

SALLAM TORQ is a modern powersports brand built around one clear idea: make performance, design and rider culture feel connected.

Our vision brings together Egypt and Italy, with an international mindset and a digital-first approach designed for a new generation of riders.

We are not limited to one category. The TORQ world can move from accessible street machines to high-performance motorcycles, while extending into scooters, racing, jets and buggies.
