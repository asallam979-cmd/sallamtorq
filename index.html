SUPPORT / FAQ

ANSWERS
BEFORE YOU BUY.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

BUY WITH CONFIDENCE.

Clear answers about parts, fitment, ordering, quotations and support — so you know exactly what happens before you confirm a request.

↓
Are the parts genuine?

We source genuine, OEM, and high-quality aftermarket parts depending on the customer's request. The exact product type is confirmed before purchase.

How do I know if a part fits my motorcycle?

Send us your brand, model, year, and the part you need. Our team checks compatibility before confirming the order.

Can I request a part that isn't listed?

Yes. If you don't see the part on the website, submit a custom request and we'll check availability.

How do I order?

Choose your motorcycle and part, submit your request, and our team will contact you on WhatsApp with availability, price, and delivery details.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery depends on availability and whether the part is locally available or imported. We'll confirm the estimated delivery time before you order.

Do you provide a quotation before payment?

Yes. You receive the price and order details before confirming the purchase.

Can I send a photo of the part I need?

Yes. You can send photos, part numbers, or your motorcycle details through WhatsApp to help us identify the correct part.

What if I order the wrong part?

We verify compatibility with you before confirming the order to reduce fitment mistakes.

Do you sell racing and performance parts?

Yes — including racing parts, protection, brakes, exhaust, carbon fiber, electronics, suspension, tires, chains and sprockets, and other performance components.

How can I contact SALLAM TORQ?

You can contact our sales team directly through WhatsApp from any product or request page.
